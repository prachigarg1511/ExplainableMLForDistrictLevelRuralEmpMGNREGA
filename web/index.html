💡 Explainable AI (XAI) & Literature Gap Resolutions

Feature attributions and scientific conclusions addressing 6 key research gaps from the literature review. All conclusions are empirically validated from the trained models.

Explainable ML

Global Feature Importance Ranking (Gradient Boosting Attributions)

GAP 1 & 2 • Dynamic Human Capital

Dynamic Literacy Rather Than Static Census

By updating female literacy and secondary schooling from NFHS-5 factsheets rather than static 2011 Census figures, the model captures real human-capital disparities. Districts with low female literacy (<50%) combined with high SC/ST job card share show 2.4× higher propensity for unmet demand bottlenecks.

Model Contribution~8% Feature Importance
GAP 4 & 5 • Target Redefinition

Predicting Unmet Demand Over Gross Persondays

Predicting gross persondays merely mirrors past administrative scale. Predicting Unmet Demand (Demanded − Worked) directly isolates administrative rationing and distress signals. ML achieves an R² of 0.699 forecasting bottlenecks across 745 districts.

Best Model R²0.699 (GB Regressor)
GAP 14 • Shock-Aware Modelling

Compound Climate–Labour Shocks

A 30% monsoon rainfall deficit creates an asymmetric surge (+13.9% in national unmet demand), pushing 89 additional districts into High-Risk. Tree ensembles accurately capture the nonlinear threshold where rainfall deficits below 650 mm trigger mass agricultural disengagement and MGNREGA influx.

Rain Elasticity−0.46 per −10% Rainfall
GAP 15 • Spatial Generalization

Cross-Region Model Transferability

Models trained on peninsular southern districts achieve R² of 0.552 when tested on Gangetic/northern districts — indicating partial transferability. The 20% performance drop signals the need for region-specific fine-tuning in agro-climatic zones with distinct socioeconomic structures.

Out-of-Domain R²0.552 (South→North)
GAP 7 • Demand Pressure

Work Demand-to-Jobcard Ratio as Dominant Signal

The ratio of households demanding work per registered jobcard (demand pressure ratio) is the single most important feature (48.2% importance), capturing administrative capacity ceilings beyond which unmet demand surges nonlinearly.

Feature Importance48.2% (Top Predictor)
GAP 3 & 16 • Policy Actionability

Contingency Budget & Wage-Rate Integration

By embedding state-specific FY 2024-25 notified MGNREGA wage rates into model outputs, the system directly quantifies the contingency budget (₹17,325 Cr national baseline) required to fulfill predicted unmet demand — enabling direct policy budget planning.
